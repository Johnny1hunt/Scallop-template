What is Scallop

The money market
Sui was built for.

Scallop is a decentralized lending and borrowing protocol on Sui. It lets you supply assets to earn yield, use those assets as collateral, and borrow with a risk engine tuned for Move’s object model. No custody, no permission — just over-collateralized markets with transparent risk.

Lending + Collateral — two tracks, one wallet

Supplying is not the same as posting collateral.

Supply to the lending pool to earn APY. Separately, deposit into a Collateral Pool to unlock borrowing power. Scallop keeps the two concerns distinct so you can compose precisely.

How it works

Three moves. No custody.

You stay in control of keys the whole time. The protocol only enforces over-collateralization.

01

Supply

Choose a lending pool — SUI, USDC, USDT, scaSUI, USDsui, XAU and more. Supply and receive the matching sCoin. It’s a standard Sui coin that steadily gains value.

Your Supply → Total Supply → Utilization → APY
↘
02

Choose how you earn

Keep the sCoin for lending yield, or deposit eligible assets into a Collateral Pool to unlock borrowing power. The two tracks are separate — you decide the mix.

Supply APY ≠ Collateral yield — distinct actions
⇌
03

Borrow, if you want

Borrow eligible assets against supported collateral up to its weight (50–85%). Keep Risk Level below 100%. Repay anytime, unwind safely — your collateral isn’t locked forever.

Risk Level is the single borrowing metric
◌
Tip: Start by supplying a stablecoin to learn the flow. You don’t have to borrow to benefit from Scallop. Risk Level explained →
